# Video content permission — action required before uploading clips

**Reminder for the project owner.** The video-segments feature cuts short clips
from third-party health educators (Luis Collantes first) and re-uploads them to
*our own* YouTube channel. That is a derivative use of someone else's content.

**Do NOT upload any clip until permission is granted in writing.** The chat
feature does not depend on it: every segment falls back to a deep link into the
original video (`watch?v=<id>&t=<start>`), which is always allowed.

## Why this matters

- Re-uploading cuts of another creator's video is copyright infringement
  without a license.
- YouTube Content ID can flag, mute, monetize-for-the-owner, or remove the
  clip; repeated claims can strike the channel.
- A polite written permission (email is enough) turns the same clip into a
  legitimate collaboration.

## What to ask for (minimum scope)

1. Permission to download, cut short segments (≤ 2–3 min) and re-upload them
   to our YouTube channel.
2. Attribution requirement accepted: clip title/description credits the
   original creator with a link to the source video.
3. Permission to keep the clip live indefinitely (or a stated time frame).
4. Confirmation that we may not alter the meaning of the content (no
   out-of-context edits).

```
- recortar fragmentos cortos (2-3 minutos) de tus videos,

- mantenerlos publicados mientras el contenido siga vigente.

En ningún caso modificaría el sentido de lo que dices, y cada clip
llevará link al video original para que tu audiencia te encuentre.

Si estás de acuerdo, basta con que respondas este correo con un "sí,
autorizado". Te agradecería mucho confirmación por escrito.

Saludos,
[nombre] — [link del sitio]
```
